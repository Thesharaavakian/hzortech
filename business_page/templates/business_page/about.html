§ 01 The mark

A sword,
not a logo.

Our mark is the letters H and T forged around a sword, with an Armenian eternity sign set into the pommel. It says what we care about better than a slogan: precision, strength, and roots in Yerevan.

It also names our method. Good systems are forged, not assembled: shaped deliberately, hardened on purpose, tested before they carry weight. Most of the problems we’re hired to fix come from the opposite — tools bolted together, infrastructure set up by hand and never written down, security added the week before launch.
